# Shareholders' Agreement Clause Library
Version: 1.0

This file defines the authoritative list of clauses that can be included in a Shareholders' Agreement. P3 prompts for this document type must reference the `clause_id`s defined here.

---

## 1. Interpretation & Parties
- **clause_id:** `sa_parties`
  - **description:** Identifies the full legal names and details of all shareholders and the company.
- **clause_id:** `sa_definitions`
  - **description:** Defines key terms used throughout the agreement (e.g., "Financial Year", "Business Day", "Fair Value").
- **clause_id:** `sa_background`
  - **description:** Briefly outlines the background of the company and the purpose of the agreement.

---

## 2. Governance and Management
- **clause_id:** `sa_board_composition`
  - **description:** Sets out the rules for the number of directors and who has the right to appoint them.
- **clause_id:** `sa_board_meetings`
  - **description:** Defines the frequency, quorum, and voting requirements for board meetings.
- **clause_id:** `sa_reserved_matters`
  - **description:** Lists key company decisions that require a special majority vote (e.g., >75%) of the board or shareholders.
- **clause_id:** `sa_director_duties`
  - **description:** Outlines the duties and responsibilities of the directors beyond their statutory duties.

---

## 3. Share Capital & Transfers
- **clause_id:** `sa_share_capital`
  - **description:** Describes the company's share capital structure.
- **clause_id:** `sa_transfer_restrictions`
  - **description:** A general clause stating that shares cannot be transferred except as permitted by the agreement.
- **clause_id:** `sa_right_of_first_refusal`
  - **description:** (ROFR) Requires a selling shareholder to first offer their shares to existing shareholders before selling to a third party.
- **clause_id:** `sa_tag_along`
  - **description:** (Co-sale right) Allows minority shareholders to participate in a sale if a majority shareholder finds a buyer for their stake.
- **clause_id:** `sa_drag_along`
  - **description:** Allows a majority shareholder to force minority shareholders to sell their shares to a third party.
- **clause_id:** `sa_permitted_transfers`
  - **description:** Defines exceptions to transfer restrictions, such as transfers to a family trust.

---

## 4. Deadlock & Dispute Resolution
- **clause_id:** `sa_deadlock_definition`
  - **description:** Defines what constitutes a deadlock situation.
- **clause_id:** `sa_deadlock_resolution_escalation`
  - **description:** Provides an escalation mechanism for resolving deadlocks (e.g., mediation, expert determination).
- **clause_id:** `sa_deadlock_shootout`
  - **description:** A terminal deadlock resolution mechanism (e.g., Texas Shootout, Russian Roulette) where one shareholder buys out the other.

---

## 5. Shareholder Obligations
- **clause_id:** `sa_confidentiality`
  - **description:** Obliges shareholders to keep company information confidential.
- **clause_id:** `sa_non_compete`
  - **description:** Restricts shareholders from competing with the company's business for a certain period.

---
